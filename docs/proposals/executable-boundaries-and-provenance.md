## 1. Executable boundary examples

AutoCedar's reviewed floors, ceilings and liveness slices are symbolic properties over an approved request universe (local paper `docs/papers/2607.03656v1.pdf`, section III-B, pages 3 to 4). Our proposed slice checks only *particular authored purchases*. It is a finite consistency check and gives no coverage or semantic-equivalence guarantee.

Current behavior: `src/leash/policy/store.py:170-178` validates the shape of agent-authored `examples[]` and accepts their claimed `expected` outcome. `src/leash/contracts/policy.py:68-71` has only description, expected and why. `app/app.js:463-478` shows those claims. Plan 01 step 3 calls for `evaluate` at draft time, and its Log explicitly says this is absent (`docs/plans/01-policy-confirmation.md:43-49,101`). The authoring guide currently asks for an allowed, forbidden and unknown example (`src/leash/policy/mcp_server.py:57-73`). A source phrase proves only textual attribution, not that an agent's rule captures the customer's intent.

**Interface for owner agreement:** retain the current display-only `examples[]` for existing drafts. Add an optional versioned `boundary_cases[]` to `PolicyDraft`, initially empty for old drafts. Each case contains `description`, `expected`, a *complete* strict `Event`, per-line `PurchaseFacts`, a complete `MandateState`, and `why`. No implicit field defaults, synthetic customer responses, scenario-specific branches or live simulator I/O. The case must identify the exact cart, amount, merchant, timestamp, policy/mandate snapshot and accepted prior approvals; `Event.mandate` must reproduce the effective confirmed rules and uncertainty policy for that case. Require `Event.authorization.mandate_id == Event.mandate.mandate_id == state.mandate_id`, matching authorization and item IDs, policy instruction and effective rule list. Fixtures remain labelled **authored examples**; their structured merchant fields and history do not authenticate a real purchase. Reject any unrepresentable requirement as an open question rather than presenting an example as verified.

At creation and revision, validate the case against `evaluate(event, candidate_policy, state, facts)` from `src/leash/engine/evaluate.py:42-98`; reject mismatched `expected`, malformed facts/provenance, or ambiguous history dependencies. Re-evaluate immediately before the customer confirms and persist the observed outcome, case-input hash and engine version with the confirmation. A change to executable case contents creates a new draft version and must be covered by the confirmed canonical hash. Because today's draft hash covers only instruction, rules and uncertainty (`src/leash/policy/store.py:88-92`), hash semantics for new drafts and migration of existing hashes require a policy/engine/app contract decision. Never silently reinterpret a previously stored hash. Boundary cases with historical familiarity need a frozen, explicit history input or must avoid history-dependent checks. `RuleContext.history` currently defaults to packaged `HISTORY` (`src/leash/engine/rules.py:45-60`), so a full reproducible historical example would require a pure evaluator input extension agreed with engine. A synthetic `Event` alone cannot disable built-in familiarity, country, duplicate and lookalike checks (`src/leash/engine/checks.py:105-173,204-217`); no case is called verified until every contributing input is explicit and reproducible.

**Minimal first demonstration after agreement:** construct one complete fixture per expected action with known facts, one forbidden amount, and one missing explicitly required fact under `uncertainty_policy=ask`; show evaluate results and rejection of a deliberately mislabeled case without a simulator request. Existing `docs/samples/scen0002_draft.json` is illustrative, not an independent intent oracle. The customer still has to verify the examples and gaps. A one-off route/MCP proposal through a temporary store and a stale-version confirmation conflict are appropriate checks; no tests, fixtures for tests, suite or linter are added.

**Touch/ownership:** contract PR first in `docs/contracts.md` and `src/leash/contracts/policy.py` (engine owns package; policy and app must agree); policy implementation in `src/leash/policy/store.py`, `confirmation.py`, `mcp_server.py` and plan 01; app display in `app/app.js` only if Rishabh approves its schema and has capacity. To avoid touching engine during held PR #47, first restrict cases to ones whose existing pure evaluator behavior is reproducible under explicit current history and only later propose `evaluate(..., history=...)`. No app or engine change goes into the policy topic worktree. Dependency: #47 changes `engine/evaluate.py`; #58 and #50 change runner and do not block a policy-only offline prototype. Estimate: 4 to 7 agent-hours for a bounded implementation with agreed schema; 9 to 14 if evaluator history injection or full migration is required. Before noon, a docs proposal and an isolated, accurately labelled offline demonstration are plausible. Customer-facing verification and engine-backed history cases belong after expert-round review.

## 2. Enforced source precedence on one conflicting claim

CaMeL isolates data from a trusted program's control flow (local `docs/papers/2503.18813v2.pdf`, sections 3 to 5). Fides uses integrity labels propagated across an agent loop and checks tools against them (`https://arxiv.org/html/2505.23643v2`, sections 4.1 to 4.4). Our proposed slice is narrower: deterministic source precedence over one typed cart-line fact. It is neither a CaMeL capability runtime nor Fides IFC/noninterference.

Current behavior: `src/leash/extract/facts.py:79-113` may choose a local catalogue's item name/category, parse merchant copy for size, return days and risk markers, and attach sources. `facts.py:120-151` currently turns unequal normalized names into `matches_request=false`. An instruction regex marks a line (`facts.py:20-29,115-118`); `src/leash/engine/evaluate.py:28-35` then erases all extracted facts for it, including any independently structured product name, while `engine/checks.py:220-226` reports uncertain. Existing `engine/checks.py:51-75` can hard-decline based on item-name mismatch or add-on markers. No part of this is a general IFC label propagation system. A matched regex is not evidence that an unrecognized injection is harmless.

**Interface for owner agreement:** keep `PurchaseFacts` and its `sources` vocabulary unchanged initially. In `extract_item`, preserve `sources.product_type=structured` only when a trusted catalogue item ID resolves; treat uncatalogued merchant item name as `merchant_text`. A line with recognized injected copy loses only merchant-text-derived fields, not independently sourced catalogue product identity or structured event item/category. Do not infer `false` for flags from absence and do not upgrade agent-form claims to `structured`. The current live runner calls `extract_event` without a catalogue (`src/leash/runner/loop.py:145-155`), so no live `product_type` has catalogue-backed `structured` provenance now: the first slice demonstrates its distinction offline but cannot claim improved live item identity without a separate runner/data-source contract. For a *direct contradiction* between a trusted catalogue item identity and merchant copy or agent-form item identity, record an explicit `uncertain` source-conflict check, not a hard mismatch until the authoritative event agrees. This requires agreeing which supplier is trusted: simulator fields are structured, but a production merchant signature is unavailable. Never call mere product-name differences a proven contradiction between a catalogue and the buyer's natural-language instruction; an unsupported synonym remains unknown and goes to an open question or customer step-up when required.

The first slice can be **only the existing injection quarantine refinement**: preserve a field with a verified `structured` source while blanking all fields with source `merchant_text` when `contains_instructions` is true, and keep the injected-instructions uncertain check. This requires no new contract or reason code. A separate conflict check would change the `Check`/reason contract and must land through its own agreed contract PR before implementation. No automatic approve is inferred from retaining the structured field: under `uncertainty_policy=approve` the existing engine maps an uncertain-only purchase to approve (`src/leash/engine/evaluate.py:76-84`), so this slice cannot promise unconditional blocking of injected text. Do not remove the explicit hard-rule failures on amount, merchant or status. On a previously quarantined line, changing the source can change evidence or outcome; run the full offline replay and report every changed row before review.

**Minimal one-off evidence:** with the current extract lane's authored corpus, compare a clean known catalogue line, an injected line whose catalogue identity remains structured while text-only return period becomes unknown, and an uncatalogued line whose merchant-supplied name remains untrusted. For the injected line, observe `injected_instructions` and its final `step_up` under `ask` absent another failing rule. A conflicting merchant phrase that the regex cannot parse stays unproven and must be described as such. No claim of general injection resistance, accuracy beyond the authored corpus or model use. Run one local `extract_item`/`evaluate` invocation and the existing offline `scripts/replay.py` once after edits; no simulator, test suite or linter.

**Touch/ownership:** `src/leash/extract/facts.py` belongs solely to the extract owner; any engine quarantine alteration in `src/leash/engine/evaluate.py` belongs solely to the engine owner and waits for #47, since that PR modifies the same file. `src/leash/engine/checks.py` and `src/leash/contracts/decision.py` are later work only if the owners accept an explicit source-conflict check. No extract writer shares the policy branch. PR #59 changes recurring-billing recognition in `facts.py`; the extract owner must rebase and coordinate the actual source lines first. Estimate: 2 to 4 agent-hours for source-preserving quarantine, 4 to 7 with a typed contradiction check and contract change. Before noon, the doc/isolated extraction observation is achievable; any engine mutation waits for #47's release and the engine owner's timing ruling.
